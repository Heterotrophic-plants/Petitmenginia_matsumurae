# Petitmenginia_matsumurae
储存钟山草（Petitmenginia matsumurae）的相关图片（植株、生境、宿主、种子、幼苗）

毛果短冠草（Sopubia matsumurae）的形态特征与短冠草属（Sopubia）的属级诊断特征明显不符。典型 Sopubia 物种具有极短的花冠筒、外露的雄蕊，以及宿存的多年生基部，可于翌年萌发多个基生分枝；而毛果短冠草则具有明显延长的花冠筒、内藏的雄蕊，不具宿存多年生基部，整体呈典型一年生形态，其整体形态更接近分布于南亚和东南亚的 Parasopubia 属。然而，Parasopubia 的花冠筒远长于毛果短冠草，两者仍存在明显差异。
更关键的区别体现在花药结构上。Sopubia 与 Parasopubia 均具有二室花药，其中仅一个药室正常发育并产生花粉，另一药室明显退化且不育；而毛果短冠草雄蕊的两个药室均发育完善，未见明显退化现象。这一稳定且具有重要分类学意义的性状使其难以归入上述任何属，提示其可能代表一条独立的演化支系，并为恢复旧属 Petitmenginia 以容纳该类群提供了形态学依据。
鉴于毛果短冠草为中国特有种，且已知野外种群预计仅存约50株，其分类地位的厘清对于制定科学、有效的保护策略具有迫切意义

Morphological traits of Sopubia matsumurae are clearly inconsistent with the generic diagnostic features of Sopubia. Typical Sopubia species possess an extremely short corolla tube, exserted stamens, and a persistent perennial base that produces multiple basal branches in the following year. In contrast, Sopubia matsumurae has a distinctly elongated corolla tube, included stamens, and lacks a persistent perennial base, exhibiting a typical annual habit. Its overall morphology is more similar to the genus Parasopubia, which is distributed in South and Southeast Asia. However, the corolla tube of Parasopubia is much longer than that of Sopubia matsumurae, and notable differences remain between the two.

An even more critical distinction lies in the anther structure. Both Sopubia and Parasopubia have two-thecous anthers, in which only one theca develops normally and produces pollen, while the other is markedly reduced and sterile. In Sopubia matsumurae, however, both thecae of each stamen are fully developed, with no obvious reduction observed. This stable character, which carries significant taxonomic weight, makes it difficult to assign this species to either of the aforementioned genera, suggesting that it may represent an independent evolutionary lineage. This provides morphological support for reinstating the old genus Petitmenginia to accommodate this taxon.

Given that Sopubia matsumurae is endemic to China and its known wild populations are estimated to number only about 50 individuals, clarifying its taxonomic status is of urgent importance for developing scientific and effective conservation strategies.
